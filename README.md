## 1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information when you use our Apps:

### 1.1 Personal Information

- **Payment Information:** If you make purchases within our Apps, we may collect payment information, such as credit card details or other financial information. This information is processed securely by our third-party payment processors.

### 1.2 Automatically Collected Information
- **Device Information:** We may collect information about your device, including the device type, operating system, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information.
- **Usage Data:** We may collect information about how you interact with our Apps, such as the features you use, the time and duration of your activities, and crash logs.
- **Location Information:** With your consent, we may collect your precise or approximate location data to provide location-based services.

### 1.3 Third-Party Information
- **Google Play Services:** If you use our Apps on an Android device, Google Play Services may collect certain information, such as your device ID and usage statistics. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for more details.
- **Other Third-Party Services:** Our Apps may integrate with third-party services (e.g., social media platforms, analytics tools) that collect information as described in their respective privacy policies.
